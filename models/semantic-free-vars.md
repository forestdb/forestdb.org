In the Rational Speech Acts framework, the literal meaning of an utterance can depend on semantic free variables—an adjective's threshold, a comparison class, a quantifier's scope, or the lexicon itself—and listeners resolve these variables jointly with the world state by reasoning pragmatically about speakers. Runnable WebPPL implementations are in the [Probabilistic Language Understanding](http://www.problang.org/) web book:

- [Vagueness: gradable adjective thresholds and comparison classes](http://www.problang.org/chapters/05-vagueness.html) (Lassiter & Goodman)
- [Jointly inferring parameters and interpretations: quantifier scope ambiguity](http://www.problang.org/chapters/04-ambiguity.html)
- [Lexical uncertainty and intended lexical meanings](http://www.problang.org/chapters/08-lexical-uncertainty.html) (Bergen, Levy & Goodman)
